# Rules (overview)

This document gives a short overview of the Hand & Foot rules as played by the family. Use this as a quick reference; specific house rules may vary and should be documented here as needed.

Core concepts
- Each player receives a "hand" and a "foot" (a second hand that is usually played after the first hand is finished).
- The goal is to form melds (sets of cards) and get rid of cards to go out, earning points for melds and bonuses.

Decks and deals
- Use multiple combined decks (commonly 4-6 decks including jokers) — project supports parameterizing deck counts.

Gameplay (high level)
- Players draw to a hand limit at the start of their turn.
- Players may lay down melds when they meet meld requirements (e.g., minimum points or card counts).
- Wild cards (jokers, twos) can be used in melds with restrictions.
- Going out requires meeting end conditions (melds and discard rules).

Scoring
- Melds score positive points; penalties for unplayed cards in hand/foot when a player goes out.
